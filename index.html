Small daily habits.
Steady energy after 40.

No extremes, no miracle fixes. Just simple routines backed by research, that fit into a real day.

Three habits to start this week

1

Water before coffee

Even mild dehydration can leave you feeling tired. A full glass of water first thing is the easiest win of the day.

Source: Harvard Health Publishing

2

Protein at breakfast

Protein helps you feel full longer, and your body uses a bit more energy to digest it than carbs or fat.

Source: Halton & Hu, Harvard School of Public Health review

3

A short walk after meals

Studies found that even 2 to 5 minutes of light walking after eating helped smooth out blood sugar rises.

Source: Buffey et al., Sports Medicine (2022)
